What is Data Acquisition?

Data Acquisition means collecting or creating the data required to train an AI model.

Imagine we want to teach an AI to identify cars. First, we need lots of useful car data!

1

🔎 Data Discovery

Finding useful data that already exists.

💡 Think about it:

Suppose we want to teach an AI to recognize cars. Do we have to take every photograph ourselves?

No! We can search for existing datasets on the Internet, databases, research collections, etc.

🔄 Data Augmentation

Creating new variations from existing data.

💡 Think about it:

Suppose we only have one car photograph. Can we create more training examples from it?

Yes! We can rotate, flip, crop or change the brightness of the image.
