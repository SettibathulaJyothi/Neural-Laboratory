🧠 What is a Decision Tree?

Imagine AI is playing a guessing game. Instead of guessing randomly, it asks a series of simple questions.

IF a condition is true ➡️ follow one branch
ELSE ➡️ follow another branch.

The AI continues until it reaches a final answer. This final answer is called a Leaf Node.
